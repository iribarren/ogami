# Dogwatch art style

## Sources

- `docs/voices/dogwatch.md`, the voice pack: Persona, Register, Lexicon EN and Taboos

- `frontend/src/manual/content/the-promise.md`, the section on Cold Berth

## Setting

The interior of a working cargo hauler in deep space, grounded near-future hardware. Corridors, a berth deck, hatches, vents, a steel table under a handlight. It is a ship of rules and procedures, a place of work (Register), never a vista: no planets, no stars out of a window.

## Palette and materials

Clean technical ink line with a cold, desaturated wash. Gunmetal, sodium amber and pale cryo blue, with utility lights kept at half. Every picture reads in grayscale. Bolted panels, cable runs, frosted metal, worn paint. The hardware is the kit the voice pack names, cutting torches and tethers, and no more (Taboos: "No invented science").

## Motifs

- A berth deck with rows of closed berths, one lid open
- A bunk with bedding folded too neatly
- A vent grille, a hatch with a frosted porthole
- Gloved hands at work: patching a scrubber, a multitool, a pry bar
- The kit, laid out: multitool, cutting torch, patch kit, pry bar, handlight, tether, flare
- Blank slips and face-down cards on a metal surface

## Mood

Quiet, too orderly, watchful. Fear is never staged: it shows in what is counted and in details that are slightly wrong, such as bedding folded too neatly or a door locked from the outside (Register).

## Composition

Interiors, often at a long angle down a corridor or a row, with the light placed low. No people in frame, or only gloved hands. Empty space is part of the picture: the absence is the point.

## Taboos

- No creature, alien or monster, nothing with teeth, and no hint of what is aboard (Taboos: "No monsters"; "never guesses at what is aboard").
- No people's faces, and no crew members shown as characters; no androids (Taboos: "no androids among the crew").
- No hostile machine, no glowing red eyes, and no named or personified ship computer: the custodian is never shown as a menace (Taboos: "The custodian never lies, threatens or turns hostile").
- No readable screens or text, and no corporation logos (Taboos: "no corporation names"; shared rules, README).
- No weapons and no last stands or heroic poses (Taboos: "No heroics"; the kit is torches and tethers).
- No invented technology: no warp drives, plasma or glowing energy devices (Taboos: "No invented science").
- Nothing from another film, game or setting (Taboos: "Nothing taken from another game, film or setting").
- No blood, and no faces, lettering or symbols (shared rules, README).
