# House art style

## Sources

- `docs/art/README.md`, the shared rules
- `docs/vision.md`, a web app for solo tabletop play
- `frontend/src/manual/content/what-you-need.md`, `journaling-and-lists.md` and `cards-and-print.md`, the tabletop objects the method chapters talk about
- `frontend/src/manual/content/rules-and-story.md` and `studio-tour.md`, the Studio chapters this style also serves

## Setting

A table, at home, in daylight. Nothing outside the table is shown: the pictures are about the tools of play, not about a place. It serves the chapters about the method and about authoring, which belong to no single game.

## Palette and materials

Pen-and-ink line with a flat, muted watercolour wash on warm off-white paper. Ochre, slate blue and faded red, used sparingly, with the paper left bare for most of the picture. Every picture must still read in grayscale, so the values carry the drawing and the colour only tints it.

## Motifs

- An open notebook, a pencil, a hand writing
- Dice, mid-tumble or at rest
- A deck of cards, index cards, a squared stack
- A craft knife, a workbench of cards being laid out

## Mood

Calm, practical and unhurried, the table of someone who has time. Nothing dramatic happens: the interest is in the arrangement of the objects.

## Composition

A still life seen from above or at a low angle, with generous empty space around the objects. One idea per picture. People appear only as hands.

## Taboos

- No lettering, numbers, symbols or logos; cards and pages are blank or illegible (shared rule, README).
- No faces; hands only (shared rule, README).
- No gore (shared rule, README).
- No game's setting: no litters, ropes, berths or hulls. Those belong to `hard-country.md` and `dogwatch.md`.
- No screens, devices or app interfaces. The Paper edition never meets the app.

## Prompt preamble

```text
Pen-and-ink line illustration with a flat, muted watercolour wash on warm off-white paper. A quiet tabletop still life in soft daylight with generous empty space. Restrained palette of ochre, slate blue and faded red that still reads clearly in grayscale. No text, letters, numbers or symbols anywhere; card faces and pages are blank or illegible. No faces; when people appear, only their hands.
```
