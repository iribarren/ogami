The Long Carry is played with the rules of Cairn, written by Yochai Gal and released under CC BY-SA 4.0. The book adapts Cairn's rules text for its rules sheet. The changes made to Cairn are listed with its rules sheet.

## 24XX

24XX rules are CC BY Jason Tocci. Cold Berth is played with an adaptation of them. The changes made to 24XX are listed with its rules sheet. No 2400 material is used.

## Inspired by

These works inspired the two flows. The book uses their ideas about situations and mood. It copies none of their text, characters or plots.

**The Long Carry**

- The story of the translation of St Cuthbert's body
- _As I Lay Dying_, William Faulkner
- _The Three Burials of Melquiades Estrada_, directed by Tommy Lee Jones

**Cold Berth**

- _Alien_, directed by Ridley Scott
- _2001: A Space Odyssey_, directed by Stanley Kubrick
- _Passengers_, directed by Morten Tyldum

Cold Berth leaves out the creature life cycle, the android-traitor reveal and the plot sequence of _Alien_.
