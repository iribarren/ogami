# The Long Carry, Complete

_Will you get the saint to the shrine before first snow, and what will it cost?_

The Long Carry is played on Hard Country, in three to four sessions. This page is the whole flow on one spread. The cards are printed separately.

## The premise

You play a small party of bearers carrying a saint's body across hard country to a shrine, before the first snow closes the passes. You are the whole table: you describe, you roll, you decide.

Two numbers keep score. **Winter** is a clock of ten boxes: the nearer it is to full, the nearer the first snow. **Burden** counts what you took on along the way: favours owed, things taken, things said aloud. Together they choose the ending.

## The Phases

| Phase           | Run    | Selection  | What happens                                                                |
| --------------- | ------ | ---------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Session Zero    | once   | `sequence` | The bearers, the saint, the vow                                             |
| The Road        | `loop` | `oracle`   | A scene from the road deck, then the World turn, until the gate is in sight |
| The Shrine Gate | once   | `sequence` | The arrival                                                                 |
| Epilogue        | once   | `sequence` | The ending, chosen by Winter and Burden                                     |

### The Road

Shuffle the road deck, with the Shrine Gate card seeded into its bottom five cards. Each round:

1. Turn the top card and play its Scene Type.
2. Do the World turn. Winter closes by one: mark a box and write how the light is different today. Then roll Road Risks.
3. Read the closing check. Winter 5 or less: play on. 6 to 9: roll a d6, and a 5 or 6 ends the Road. 10 ends it.

The Road also ends when the Shrine Gate card turns up.

## The Scene Types

| Scene Type      | Purpose                                                              |
| --------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| The bearers     | Make the people who will carry the saint                             |
| The saint       | Say whose body you carry and where it is going                       |
| The vow         | Say what you promised and what you will pay if you break it          |
| Camp            | Stop, rest and say what cannot be said while walking                 |
| Ford            | Cross water, and learn what the crossing costs                       |
| Stranger        | Meet someone who wants something from the road, or from you          |
| Ruin            | Find what earlier travellers left, and decide whether to take it     |
| Omen            | Let the road say something about the saint, and decide what it means |
| The Shrine Gate | Arrive, and be asked what you carried                                |
| Epilogue        | Read what the road made of you                                       |

The road deck holds thirteen cards: three Camps, two Fords, three Strangers, two Ruins and three Omens.

## Trackers

| Tracker | Kind    | Range  | Moves                                                          |
| ------- | ------- | ------ | -------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Winter  | clock   | 10     | Up one every World turn, and by some Road Risks                |
| Burden  | counter | 0 to 6 | Up by hard choices and checks that go wrong, and by a bad foot |

## Fact slots

- **The saint:** whose body you carry. Written in Session Zero.
- **The Sin:** what the saint did, as you now believe it. Each Omen can change it. The last Sin you write is the Truth.
- **The vow:** what you promised, and to whom. Written in Session Zero.

## The tables

Road Risks is rolled in every World turn. Most results are quiet; the rest cost a point of Winter or Burden, or force a Ford, a Stranger, a Ruin or an Omen as the next scene. A risen ford calls for a Back check. Three more tables, Strangers, Omens and Ruins, give ideas for their scenes.

## The Truth

The Truth is emergent. Nobody tells you what the saint did. Each Omen asks what you now believe, and you write the Sin again.

## The endings

The ending is chosen by Winter and Burden, not by you. In time is Winter 7 or less; light is Burden 2 or less.

| Winter  | Burden | Ending                    |
| ------- | ------ | ------------------------- |
| In time | Light  | Laid down in time         |
| In time | Heavy  | Laid down at a price      |
| Late    | Light  | Laid down at a price      |
| Late    | Heavy  | The carry is not finished |

Your part is the aftermath: what each bearer does next.
