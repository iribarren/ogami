# Hard Country Rules

Hard Country is a rules-light fantasy game about hard travel, where a body gives out before a story does. It has a single sheet, for a bearer, and The Long Carry is played on that sheet. The cards are printed separately.

## Making a bearer

- **Name and calling.** Choose a name, and a calling that says what you did before the carry, such as a ferryman, a nun or a deserter.
- **Traits.** Roll 2d6+3 for each of Back, Feet and Head, the three traits.

| Trait | Used to                         |
| ----- | ------------------------------- |
| Back  | Carry, fight, endure            |
| Feet  | Move, dodge, sneak              |
| Head  | Notice, reason, hold your nerve |

- **Wind.** Roll d6+2 for Wind, which is your breath and your nerve together. It is never above 8.
- **Pennies.** Roll 2d6 and multiply the result by 10.
- **Load.** You have eight slots to carry things in.

## Checks

When you try something, name the trait that fits it and roll a d20. A roll at or under the trait succeeds, a 1 is clean and a 20 goes badly.

| Roll                  | Result        |
| --------------------- | ------------- |
| 1                     | Clean success |
| At or under the trait | Success       |
| Over the trait        | Failure       |
| 20                    | Goes badly    |

The flow says what a failure or a roll that goes badly will cost, and the cost it names is usually a Tracker or Wind.

## Harm

Harm takes Wind first. Once you have no Wind left, each harm is a **Wound** instead, and you mark a box for it. Three Wounds put you down, and Wounds do not heal. Wind does come back, but only when you rest in Camp, where you roll d6+2 and recover that much Wind, up to 8.

## Load

::image{file="hc-kit.webp" style="hard-country" size="half" alt="The bearers' kit laid out on a blanket: rope, lantern, staff, waterskin, bandages, knife and sling." subject="The kit laid out on a rough wool blanket: a coil of rope, a lantern, a staff, a waterskin, rolled bandages, a knife and a sling."}

You carry eight slots. Most things take one slot each, heavy things such as the carrying poles and the pole axe take two, and lamp oil takes none.

| Kit            | Cost | Slots |
| -------------- | ---- | ----- |
| Rope           | 8    | 1     |
| Lantern        | 12   | 1     |
| Lamp oil       | 2    | 0     |
| Staff          | 3    | 1     |
| Blanket        | 6    | 1     |
| Bandages       | 4    | 1     |
| Waterskin      | 3    | 1     |
| Trail rations  | 2    | 1     |
| Carrying poles | 10   | 2     |

| Arms     | Cost | Slots |
| -------- | ---- | ----- |
| Knife    | 5    | 1     |
| Club     | 2    | 1     |
| Sling    | 4    | 1     |
| Pole axe | 20   | 2     |

Costs are in pennies. Bandages close a cut, but they do not mend a Wound.
