# The two games

The book is built around two games and one flow for each. A **GameSystem** holds a game's rules: its dice, its checks, its character sheet. A **NarrativeFlow** is one story inside that GameSystem. One system can hold many flows; here each has one.

## The Long Carry, on Cairn

_Will you get the saint to the shrine before first snow, and what will it cost?_

Cairn is a rules-light fantasy game. Its characters are fragile, its fights are dangerous, and its rules fit on a few pages. The Long Carry runs for three to four sessions.

You play a small group of bearers carrying a saint's body across hard country.

- **Session Zero** sets the bearers, the saint and the vow that sent you.
- **The Road** repeats. A shuffled deck of scenes decides what you meet next: a camp, a ford, a stranger, a ruin, an omen. The last scene is hidden near the bottom of the deck. Each round the world turn moves the **Winter** clock and rolls a small table of road risks.
- **The Shrine Gate** is the arrival.
- **The Epilogue** has three endings, chosen by Winter and by the **Burden** you carry.

The Truth of the story, what the saint's sin was, is emergent. You write it as the omens pile up.

## Cold Berth, on 24XX

_What is aboard, who let it on, and why were we woken?_

24XX is a minimal science-fiction game. A character is a few numbers, and a check is a single die. Cold Berth runs for two to three sessions.

You play crew members woken early from cold sleep on a ship that should be empty.

- **Session Zero** gives each of you a crew card and seals three Truths in the notebook.
- **Wake** is the empty berth and the custodian's refusal to explain.
- **The Ship** repeats. You choose what to do (investigate, repair, confront or hide) and then which compartment to go to. Each round the world turn raises the **Threat** clock and picks a crew member who is missing.
- **Reveal** opens the sealed Truths.
- **The Epilogue** says who gets out, by the **Clues** you found and the Threat that remains.

Here the Truth is sealed, written before play and opened at the end.

## Why two

The two flows show opposite choices. One lets the Truth appear as you play and the other hides it from the start. One shuffles scenes into a deck, one lets you pick. Each chapter shows an idea with both, and you can decide which suits the story you want.
