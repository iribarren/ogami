# What you need

A solo game needs very little. Gather these before the first session.

## Always

- **A pencil and a notebook.** The notebook is your memory. You write in it the answers you invent, the names you meet and the clocks you start. Any paper works.
- **Polyhedral dice, d4 to d20.** One each of d4, d6, d8, d10, d12 and d20. A second d6 and a second d20 are useful. A dice roller on your phone is an acceptable substitute.
- **A deck of playing cards.** A standard 52-card deck. The book uses it as an oracle and as a shuffled stack of scenes.
- **Index cards.** About fifty. Each scene, each person and each place of your flow gets a card, so that you can draw them, stack them and shuffle them.

## Helpful

- A few coloured pens, to tell your clocks and threads apart.
- Paper clips or a small box, to keep the face-up and face-down piles apart.
- A timer, if you like sessions of a set length.

## How long

A first session takes about an hour, and the two example flows run for two to four sessions each. Stop at a natural pause, such as the end of a scene or a phase, rather than when a set time runs out.

## Where you play

You can play anywhere you have room to spread out a few cards. The game needs your attention and a quiet table, but it does not need a game master, since you hold the pencil and the book gives the questions.
